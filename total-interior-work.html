Total Interior Work

From the first conversation to the final handover, one coordinated plan for your complete interior project. The exact scope is confirmed after a site visit and written quotation.

Complete project coverage

From empty space to finished space.

We help coordinate the key parts of your project so design decisions, materials and execution work together.

  • Site measurement and requirement discussion
  • Space planning, layout and design concepts
  • 3D views and design revisions where included
  • Material, colour, lighting and finish selection
  • Civil changes and surface preparation, if agreed
  • False ceiling, electrical and lighting coordination
  • Modular kitchen, wardrobes and custom carpentry
  • Painting, wall finishes, furniture and styling
  • Quality checks, snag list and final handover
Every project has a different scope. Work items, brands, quantities, timeline, taxes and exclusions should be written in the final quotation before work begins.
