Simple, collaborative, clear

Good work starts with a good plan.

Our process is designed to keep your priorities visible and decisions manageable.

01

Listen & understand

We learn how you use the space, your style preferences and your budget range.

02

Measure & plan

We review the site, measurements, circulation and practical requirements.

03

Design & approve

We discuss concepts, finishes and a written scope before execution.

04

Build & coordinate

Agreed work is coordinated with progress updates and checks.

05

Review & hand over

We review the completed work, list any snags and confirm handover.

Communication matters

No surprises, just clear conversations.

Written scope

Know what's included, what's excluded and what each item costs.

Approvals

Confirm important design and material choices before ordering.

Progress updates

Stay informed about milestones, dependencies and next steps.
