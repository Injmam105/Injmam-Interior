## How to preview
Extract the ZIP, then double-click `index.html`. Internet access is needed to load the remote Unsplash photos.

## Important before publishing
- Replace the demo WhatsApp number `917503354996` in all HTML files and `assets/script.js`.
- Replace Instagram and Facebook URLs with your exact business profile links.
- Replace the sample email and service area.
- Gallery and portfolio images are illustrative remote images, not verified photos of your own completed projects. Swap them for your own work photographs before advertising.
- The contact form opens WhatsApp with the details; it does not store submissions on a server.
- For a live public website, upload the folder to hosting such as GitHub Pages, Netlify or your own domain hosting.
